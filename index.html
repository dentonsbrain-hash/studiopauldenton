Inside the world of Paul Denton

About

Paul Denton is a British designer and artist creating collectible objects that explore the intersection of design, narrative, and cultural mythology.

He studied Product Design at the University of Northampton before completing a Master’s degree in Vehicle Design at the Royal College of Art. Denton spent close to a decade at Ron Arad’s studio, working across design and digital sculpture, where he developed a deep understanding of experimental design, material form, and gallery culture.

Alongside his independent practice, he has spent over eleven years at GA Design Group contributing to award-winning international hospitality projects, refining a precision-led approach to form, atmosphere, and visual storytelling.

Influenced by thinkers such as Rupert Sheldrake, Jacques Vallée, and Diana Pasulka, Denton’s work explores objects as speculative artefacts positioned between the rational and the mythological. Through limited editions and material-driven processes, his work investigates how objects can operate simultaneously as design, evidence, and cultural signal.

Currently developing a debut series of limited-edition collectible works, The first collection will launch in 2026.
